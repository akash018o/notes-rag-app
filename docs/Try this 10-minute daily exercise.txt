Try this 10-minute daily exercise

For the next month:

5 minutes — observation

Sit somewhere and notice 10 details around you. Leave the area and try to recall all 10.

3 minutes — yesterday recall

Without looking at your phone/calendar, reconstruct yesterday from morning → afternoon → evening.

2 minutes — learning recall

Learn 3–5 small facts, close the source, and recall them. Test yourself again tomorrow.

After a few weeks, gradually increase the difficulty.

And don't get discouraged if you forget. Forgetting is actually part of the training. Every time you successfully retrieve something that was becoming difficult to remember, you're practicing the exact skill you want to improve.
